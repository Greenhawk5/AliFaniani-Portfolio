## Loading Assets

Two animated SVG loading visuals in this project are derived from
[Circleloaders](https://circleloaders.dominikakissi.com/#top), a set of
twenty-four monochrome circular loaders drawn by **Domi Kissi** (web & graphic
designer).

- Source: https://circleloaders.dominikakissi.com/#top
- Author: Domi Kissi (https://dominikakissi.com)
- Affected files: `src/components/room/LoadingVeil.tsx` (Volute loader,
  set entry #21) and `src/components/home/GooExperienceIcon.tsx`
  (goo-style loader, adapted and recolored to the project's accent).

No explicit license or terms-of-use statement is published on the
Circleloaders website; the author invites visitors to copy the loaders from
the site. Accordingly, **this repository does not independently grant rights
over these SVG assets**, and the project's LICENSE does not apply to them.
Anyone wishing to reuse these loading visuals should consult the author
directly.
